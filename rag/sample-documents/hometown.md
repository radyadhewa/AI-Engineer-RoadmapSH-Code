The historic district preserves several 19th- and early-20th-century buildings, including the old courthouse, the market hall, and several churches.

-------------------

- Climate: temperate with four seasons — mild springs, warm summers, colorful autumns, and cool winters. Average annual rainfall is moderate. Snow is rare to occasional depending on elevation.
- Landscape features: river valley, minor hills, some protected woodland areas and parks.
